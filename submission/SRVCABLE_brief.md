# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE) — research brief

As of 2026-09-23

## Snapshot

Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors. [S03](#source-s03)
For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, revenue from operations reached 1,248 crore rupees, an increase of 18.0 percent year over year. [S03](#source-s03)
Profit after tax for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 rose 5.9 percent year over year to 82 crore rupees. [S03](#source-s03)

## Bull case

- The order book stood at 3,900 crore rupees as of 30 June 2026, compared with 3,150 crore rupees a year prior. [S03](#source-s03)
- As of 30 June 2026, promoters held 58.2 percent of shares, and pledged shares represented 4.1 percent of the promoter holding. [S04](#source-s04) [S08](#source-s08)
- A new plant in Bharuch is scheduled to add about 30 percent to cable capacity if commercial production begins as planned in Q3 FY27. [S03](#source-s03)

## Bear case

- Receivable days, measuring how long customers take to pay, lengthened from 78 to 96 over the preceding year due to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S02](#source-s02)
- Margin contraction occurred because of higher copper costs, while price variation clauses cover only about two thirds of contracts and reset with a lag of one to two quarters. [S02](#source-s02) [S03](#source-s03)
- The company received an order demanding 46.3 crore rupees, including interest and penalty, regarding input tax credit claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S07](#source-s07)

## Open questions

- What caused a secondary news report to show Q1 FY27 revenue of 1,428 crore rupees instead of the 1,248 crore rupees reported in the company press release? [S01](#source-s01) [S03](#source-s03)
- Has the company filed an appeal against the 46.3 crore rupee tax demand order, and what is the current status of that legal challenge? [S07](#source-s07)
- Will the two state utility customers settle their balances soon, or will delayed collections continue to stretch working capital? [S02](#source-s02)
