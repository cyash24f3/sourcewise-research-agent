# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE) — research brief

As of 2026-09-23

## Snapshot

Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors. [S03](#source-s03)
In Q1 FY27 ended 30 June 2026, revenue rose 18.0% year over year to 1,248 crore rupees, and EBITDA rose 2.6% to 140 crore rupees. [S03](#source-s03)
Net profit rose 5.9% to 82 crore rupees, while EBITDA margin narrowed by 170 basis points to 11.2%. [S03](#source-s03)

## Bull case

- The order book reached 3,900 crore rupees by 30 June 2026, while exports contributed 21% of quarterly revenue and data-centre cables provided 9% of domestic sales, which management expects could double in two years. [S02](#source-s02) [S03](#source-s03)
- The planned Bharuch plant is expected to add about 30% cable capacity in Q3 FY27, with about 70% of its 620 crore rupee budget spent, modest net debt to equity of 0.35 times, and maintained annual revenue growth guidance of 15% to 17%. [S02](#source-s02) [S03](#source-s03)
- Pledged promoter shares dropped to 4.1% of the 58.2% promoter holding as of 30 June 2026, down from 11.5% a year prior. [S04](#source-s04) [S08](#source-s08)

## Bear case

- EBITDA margin narrowed by 170 basis points following a nearly 14% rise in copper costs; while management expects to pass through higher prices, about two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses resetting with a one-to-two-quarter lag. [S01](#source-s01) [S02](#source-s02) [S03](#source-s03)
- Time taken to collect payments rose from 78 to 96 days over the past year due to delays from two state utility clients, though management affirmed no defaults have occurred. [S02](#source-s02)
- On 2 September 2026, the company received a 46.3 crore rupee GST demand order; while management intends to appeal and expects no material impact, the liability remains pending. [S07](#source-s07)

## Open questions

- What caused secondary media to report Q1 FY27 revenue of 1,428 crore rupees, conflicting with the primary company disclosure of 1,248 crore rupees? [S01](#source-s01) [S03](#source-s03)
- How quickly can management normalize cash collections from the two state utility customers to reduce payment collection times from 96 days? [S02](#source-s02)
- When will management formally submit its planned appeal against the 46.3 crore rupee tax demand, and will Bharuch commercial operations launch on schedule in Q3 FY27? [S03](#source-s03) [S07](#source-s07)
