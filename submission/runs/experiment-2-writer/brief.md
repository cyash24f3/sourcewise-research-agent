# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE) — research brief

As of 2026-09-23

## Snapshot

For Q1 FY27, Sarvottam Cables reported consolidated revenue of 1,248 crore rupees (up 18.0 percent year on year), operating profit before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) of 140 crore rupees (up 2.6 percent), and net profit of 82 crore rupees (up 5.9 percent), with primary exchange filings taking precedence over a secondary news article reporting 1,428 crore rupees. [S01](#source-s01) [S03](#source-s03)
EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points year on year to 11.2 percent, a compression that management primarily attributed to rising copper prices. [S02](#source-s02) [S03](#source-s03)
As of 30 June 2026, the company held an order book of 3,900 crore rupees, while promoter pledging stood at 4.1 percent of promoter holding, updating and declining from older historical news reports. [S03](#source-s03) [S04](#source-s04) [S08](#source-s08)

## Bull case

- Commercial demand remains positive, with the total order book expanding to 3,900 crore rupees as of 30 June 2026 from 3,150 crore rupees a year earlier and exports rising to 21 percent of revenue compared to 16 percent in Q1 FY26. [S03](#source-s03)
- Management reaffirmed full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15 percent to 17 percent, supported by a 620 crore rupee plant in Bharuch, Gujarat, that is already 70 percent spent and expected to increase cable capacity by about 30 percent when commercial operations begin in Q3 FY27. [S02](#source-s02) [S03](#source-s03)
- Data-centre cables accounted for 9 percent of domestic sales with management projecting this share to double in two years, while management expects to recover recent cost pressure over the next two quarters because two-thirds of contracts possess price-variation clauses adjusting with a one-to-two-quarter lag. [S02](#source-s02) [S03](#source-s03)

## Bear case

- Receivable days rose from 78 to 96 over the past year, showing that customers are taking longer to settle invoices, primarily due to slower payment cycles from two large state utility clients. [S02](#source-s02)
- Operating margins fell 170 basis points year on year due to copper costs, leaving profitability vulnerable in the interim because price adjustments reset with a one-to-two-quarter lag and approximately one-third of contracts lack price-variation terms entirely. [S02](#source-s02) [S03](#source-s03)
- The company disclosed a tax demand order on 2 September 2026 for 46.3 crore rupees including interest and penalty regarding past input tax credits, representing a material liability where an appeal has not yet been decided despite management expectations. [S07](#source-s07)

## Open questions

- What accounts for the reporting discrepancy between the company filing showing Q1 FY27 revenue of 1,248 crore rupees and the secondary press report citing 1,428 crore rupees? [S01](#source-s01) [S03](#source-s03)
- Will upcoming filings show cash collection timelines from the two state utility customers improving, or will payment delays beyond 96 receivable days lead to formal bad-debt provisions? [S02](#source-s02)
- When will the company formally file its appeal against the 46.3 crore rupee GST demand order, and will any mandatory cash pre-deposit be required during the appellate process? [S07](#source-s07)
