## Conflicts and temporal updates

### Q1 FY27 revenue from operations and growth rate

Use the official company press release (S03), which reports Q1 FY27 revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY). The news article (S01) reports ₹1,428 crore (+35% YoY); the reason for the secondary source's discrepancy is unverified.

[S01](<https://businessdaily.example/markets/sarvottam-cables-q1-results>)

> Revenue from operations rose to \*\*₹1,428 crore\*\*, a 35% increase from the year-ago period, the company said in an exchange filing on Friday.

[S03](<https://ir.sarvottamcables.example/press/q1fy27>)

> \| Revenue from operations \| 1,248 \| 1,058 \| +18.0% \|

### Promoter pledge percentage

This represents a temporal update rather than a same-period conflict. In March 2024, promoter pledge was reported at 35% of promoter holding (S04), whereas official shareholding disclosures as of June 30, 2026 (S08) show promoter pledge declined to 4.1% of promoter holding.

[S04](<https://marketwatchindia.example/2024/03/sarvottam-promoter-pledge>)

> The promoters of Sarvottam Cables Ltd have increased the share of their holding that is pledged with lenders to 35%, up from 22% six months ago, according to exchange data.

[S08](<https://nse.example/shareholding/SRVCABLE/2026-06>)

> \| Of which pledged (as % of promoter holding) \| 4.1% \| 6.0% \| 11.5% \|
