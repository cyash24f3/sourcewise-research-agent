# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE) — research brief

As of 2026-09-23

## Snapshot

Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures and supplies power, export, and domestic data-centre cables. [S02](#source-s02) [S03](#source-s03)
For Q1 FY27, primary filings report consolidated revenue from operations of 1,248 crore rupees, an 18.0 percent year-over-year increase, and net profit of 82 crore rupees. [S03](#source-s03)
Operating EBITDA reached 140 crore rupees for the quarter, while the operating margin contracted by 170 basis points year over year to 11.2 percent. [S02](#source-s02) [S03](#source-s03)

## Bull case

- The company held an order book of 3,900 crore rupees as of 30 June 2026 with export revenue expanding to 21 percent, while management maintained full-year revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent. [S02](#source-s02) [S03](#source-s03)
- The new Bharuch plant will expand cable capacity by approximately 30 percent, with commercial production scheduled for Q3 FY27 and roughly 70 percent of its 620 crore rupee budget already spent alongside a conservative net debt to equity ratio of 0.35 times. [S02](#source-s02) [S03](#source-s03)
- Higher-value data-centre cables represent approximately 9 percent of domestic sales, a revenue stream that management expects could double within two years. [S02](#source-s02)

## Bear case

- Profitability declined as the EBITDA margin fell to 11.2 percent due to higher copper costs; although management indicated about two-thirds of contracts allow charging customers for increased input costs, these adjustments face a lag of one to two quarters. [S02](#source-s02) [S03](#source-s03)
- Working capital requirements increased as the time taken to collect payments from clients lengthened from 78 to 96 days due to slower payment cycles from two state utility customers, though management confirmed no defaults have occurred. [S02](#source-s02)
- On 2 September 2026, the company received a tax demand order of 46.3 crore rupees concerning historical input tax credits; management stated an intention to appeal and expects no material impact, though the dispute remains unresolved. [S07](#source-s07)

## Open questions

- What accounts for the discrepancy where secondary news reported Q1 FY27 revenue of 1,428 crore rupees with 35 percent growth, whereas the primary exchange filing reported 1,248 crore rupees and 18.0 percent growth? [S01](#source-s01) [S03](#source-s03)
- What is the filing status, hearing schedule, and potential cash liability if the appeal against the 46.3 crore rupee GST demand order is not decided in the company's favour? [S07](#source-s07)
- What concrete milestones and timing do management expect for recovering delayed payments from the two state utilities to reverse the rise in collection days from 96 back toward 78? [S02](#source-s02)
